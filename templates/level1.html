What You Learned

  • Strong passwords should avoid names, birthdays, and personal information
  • Numbers and symbols improve password complexity
  • Longer and less predictable passwords are harder to crack
  • Common words and simple patterns make passwords weak
  • Unique passwords are safer than easy-to-guess passwords

Real-Life Safety Advice

Use a different password for each important account. Avoid names, birthdays, “123”, or common words. If possible, use a password manager to create and store strong passwords safely.
