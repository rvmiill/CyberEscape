Malware Types

🦠 Virus

Attaches to files or programs and spreads when they are opened.

🌐 Worm

Spreads automatically through a network without user action.

🎭 Trojan

Pretends to be safe or useful software but hides malware.

🔒 Ransomware

Locks or encrypts files and demands payment.

👁️ Spyware

Secretly monitors users and steals private information.
